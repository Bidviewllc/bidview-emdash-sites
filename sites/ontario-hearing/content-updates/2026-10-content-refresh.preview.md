## /over-the-counter-hearing-aids/

Meta title: Over-the-Counter Hearing Aids | Ontario Hearing Center
Meta description: Over-the-counter hearing aids need no prescription if you are 18 or older with mild to moderate hearing loss. See who they fit and how they compare with prescription aids.

![Hand holding beige behind-the-ear hearing aid with case](/assets/img/woman-holding-hearing-aid-closeup-1-f77ba69d.webp)

You can buy over-the-counter (OTC) hearing aids without a prescription if you are 18 or older and have mild to moderate hearing loss. The [FDA](https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/hearing-aids/otc-hearing-aids-what-you-should-know) created the category in October 2022 to improve accessibility, and you can buy these devices in a store or online.

Ontario Hearing Center is an audiology practice in Brighton, and we have served the Rochester area since 1956. If you are deciding between an OTC aid and a prescription aid, our audiologists can explain what your hearing test results mean for each.

## Do over-the-counter hearing aids work?

They work for adults whose hearing loss is mild to moderate and about the same in both ears. The FDA lists these signs of that level of loss:

- Trouble hearing speech in noisy places
- Trouble following conversations in a group
- Trouble hearing on the phone
- Feeling tired after listening
- A TV or radio turned up louder than others like

Individuals with severe or profound hearing loss need a prescription aid. Children and teens under 18 need one too, and a child with hearing trouble needs a doctor, preferably an ear, nose and throat doctor. An [audiologist](https://ontariohearing.com/about-us/) fits and programs prescription aids.

See a doctor before you buy hearing aids if you have ear pain, fluid or blood coming from the ear, a sudden change in hearing, dizziness, hearing that is worse in one ear, or ringing in only one ear.

## Over-the-counter vs prescription hearing aids

The main difference is who sets the aid up. You fit an OTC aid yourself with the instructions and a phone app. A licensed hearing professional programs a prescription aid to your hearing test results.

|   | OTC hearing aids | Prescription hearing aids |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Who they are for | Adults 18 and older with mild to moderate hearing loss | Every level of hearing loss and every age |
| Who fits them | You, with the instructions and an app | A licensed hearing professional, programmed to your results |
| Follow-up care | Varies by seller | Often part of the price |

A hearing test shows your level of loss, whether it is about the same in both ears, and whether a medical issue needs attention first.

## Features and technology

OTC aids come in two kinds. Basic models have volume control and preset programs, and self-fitting models add an app for adjustments. Either kind can connect to a phone. OTC feature lists vary by model, so check the box for the battery type. For example, some self-fitting aids need a phone app, so confirm it works with yours.

An audiologist programs prescription aids based on your hearing test results. Hearing aids we fit at Ontario Hearing Center offer:

- Bluetooth connection to your phone
- Rechargeable batteries
- Background noise reduction
- Tinnitus support

We fit aids from Oticon, Widex, Unitron, ReSound, Phonak, and Starkey.

We program each device to your results and check it with real-ear measurement.

## Disadvantages of over-the-counter hearing aids

OTC aids have limited power, and no hearing test or professional fitting is required to buy one.

OTC aids are built for mild to moderate hearing loss, so their maximum volume is limited. If your loss is bigger than you think, the aid can fall short.

You can buy an OTC aid without a hearing test, so you can end up with one without knowing your level of loss. At our office, a hearing test with an audiologist shows the type and degree of your loss, and we fit prescription aids to those results.

You also set up an OTC aid yourself, with an app and instructions. Prescription aids offer more customization, and a professional checks the fit and the settings. Our audiologists include real-ear measurement in the fitting. With [real ear measurement](https://ontariohearing.com/real-ear-measurement/), we can adjust the aid to your ear instead of relying on the manufacturer’s preset.

## Frequently asked questions

### Can I buy a hearing aid without a prescription?

Yes, if you are 18 or older and have mild to moderate hearing loss. OTC hearing aids are sold in stores and online without a prescription, an exam, or a fitting by an audiologist. Anyone under 18, and anyone with severe or profound hearing loss needs a prescription aid from a licensed hearing professional.

### Are OTC hearing aids effective?

They help adults whose hearing loss is mild to moderate. They fall short for a bigger loss because their maximum volume is limited. A hearing test shows which group you are in.

### How do OTC hearing aids compare to prescription ones?

You fit an OTC aid yourself. A licensed hearing professional or audiologist programs a prescription aid to your hearing test results, and follow-up care is often part of the price

### Are OTC hearing aids covered by insurance?

Original Medicare does not cover hearing aids, OTC, or prescription. Some Medicare Advantage plans do, and coverage varies, so ask your plan whether it covers OTC aids. You can pay for either kind with HSA or FSA funds.

### Should I get a hearing test before buying an OTC hearing aid?

The FDA does not require one. A test is the way to find out your level of loss, whether it is about the same in both ears, and whether a medical issue needs attention first. In Rochester, you do not need a referral for a hearing test unless Medicare is your primary insurance. Almost all insurance plans cover testing, minus a possible co-pay, so check with your insurer.

## /how-to-cure-tinnitus-naturally/

Meta title: How to Cure Tinnitus Naturally | Ontario Hearing Center
Meta description: There is no proven natural cure for tinnitus, but some habits ease it. See what the guidelines support and why a hearing evaluation comes first.

![Doctor pointing to anatomical ear model while patient takes notes](/assets/img/hands-showing-structure-artificial-human-ear-4a0dc7b4.webp)

There is no proven natural cure for tinnitus. Some habits make it less noticeable, and an audiologist can find out what is behind it and which treatments have evidence.

Ontario Hearing Center is an audiology practice in Brighton. We provide [tinnitus evaluation and management](https://ontariohearing.com/tinnitus/), and we have served the Rochester area since 1956.

## Which natural remedies help tinnitus?

Natural remedies are habits that make tinnitus easier to live with: protecting your ears from loud sound, adding background sound, managing stress, sleeping enough, and watching caffeine and alcohol. They do not remove the sound.

### Protect your ears from loud noise

Loud noise causes tinnitus and makes it worse. Wear earplugs or earmuffs at concerts and around machinery, and keep headphone volume low.

We make custom ear protection for musicians, sleep, and swimming.

### Add background sound

A steady background sound makes tinnitus less noticeable, especially in a quiet room at night. A white noise machine, a fan, or soft music can all work. Tinnitus sound generators work the same way. They look like hearing aids and make the tinnitus seem less loud.

## How do stress and sleep affect tinnitus?

Stress and fatigue make tinnitus seem louder. Deep breathing, regular exercise, and enough sleep help you manage both. A white noise machine in the bedroom helps if the ringing keeps you awake.

Yoga and meditation can help you relax. Among treatments aimed at tinnitus itself, the [2026 AAO-HNSF update](https://www.epocrates.com/online/article/tinnitus-guideline-update-shifts-focus-toward-individualized-care-away-from-drugs) specifically recommends only cognitive behavioral therapy (CBT). A trained provider delivers CBT, and it focuses on how much tinnitus bothers you.

## Can diet affect tinnitus?

Diet changes how noticeable tinnitus is for some people. No diet cures it. Some people hear more ringing after caffeine or alcohol.

The research on caffeine is mixed. In one study, women who drank more caffeine were less likely to hear ringing. If you notice more ringing after coffee, cut back for a few weeks and see whether it changes.

## Do supplements work for tinnitus?

No supplement has been shown to treat tinnitus, and the guidelines recommend against them. The [2014 AAO-HNSF guideline](https://eguideline.guidelinecentral.com/i/394498/2) named ginkgo biloba, melatonin, and zinc, and the [2026 update](https://www.epocrates.com/online/article/tinnitus-guideline-update-shifts-focus-toward-individualized-care-away-from-drugs) again recommends against routine use of dietary supplements.

Tell your doctor before you take any supplement or herb.

## What treatments do the guidelines support?

The [2026 AAO-HNSF](https://www.newswise.com/articles/living-with-ringing-in-your-ears-updated-guideline-supports-a-personalized-evidence-based-approach-to-tinnitus) update says every patient with tinnitus should have a comprehensive hearing evaluation. For persistent, bothersome tinnitus, it points to cognitive behavioral therapy, hearing aids, and sound enrichment. It covers adults whose tinnitus lasts six months or longer and affects their quality of life.

No single approach works for everyone. The [2014 guideline](https://guidelinecentral.com/guideline/6931) recommends a hearing aid evaluation for people who have both hearing loss and tinnitus.

## What causes tinnitus?

Tinnitus has many causes, including hearing loss, loud noise exposure, earwax, some medicines, and head or neck injuries.

Medicines that can cause tinnitus include high doses of aspirin, aminoglycoside antibiotics, loop diuretics, quinine, and platinum-based chemotherapy drugs. Other pain relievers, such as ibuprofen and naproxen, are also linked to it.

Tinnitus from a medicine often fades after it is stopped, but chemotherapy drugs and some antibiotics can cause lasting tinnitus. Do not stop a prescribed medicine on your own. Ask the doctor who prescribed it.

## Why a tinnitus evaluation comes first

An audiologist finds out whether hearing loss, earwax, or something else is behind your tinnitus, then builds a plan around it. Tinnitus often goes with hearing loss, and a hearing test shows whether you have it.

Your evaluation includes a comprehensive hearing test and questions about your tinnitus: when you notice it, what it sounds like, how loud it is, and whether it affects your sleep or concentration. Management can then include hearing aids with tinnitus support, sound enrichment, and counseling, depending on what the evaluation shows.

Every patient at Ontario Hearing Center is seen by a doctoral-level audiologist. Audiologists and ENT doctors work together on tinnitus. If everyday sounds also feel painfully loud, we evaluate sound sensitivity, called hyperacusis.

## When to see a doctor first

The guidelines call for a prompt hearing evaluation when tinnitus is in one ear, lasts six months or longer, or comes with hearing difficulty. Get a prompt hearing evaluation and a doctor’s visit if your tinnitus:

- Is in one ear only
- Pulses with your heartbeat
- Started suddenly or came with a change in hearing
- Comes with dizziness, or with weakness or numbness
- Started after a head or neck injury
- Started after a new medicine
- Follows a cold and lasts longer than about a week

## Frequently asked questions

### Can diet affect tinnitus symptoms?

For some people, yes. Caffeine, alcohol, and nicotine can change how noticeable the ringing is, and the research on caffeine is mixed. No diet cures tinnitus.

### Are there exercises to relieve tinnitus?

Regular exercise helps with stress and sleep, which affect how noticeable tinnitus is. No exercise treats tinnitus itself, and the guidelines do not list one.

### How does stress management help tinnitus?

Stress makes tinnitus seem louder. Relaxation techniques and CBT reduce the stress. CBT, delivered by a trained provider, is the behavioral treatment the guidelines recommend for tinnitus that bothers you.

### What supplements are effective for tinnitus?

None. The AAO-HNSF recommends against routine use of dietary supplements for tinnitus, including ginkgo biloba, melatonin, and zinc. Tell your doctor before you take any.

## /what-are-the-first-signs-of-tinnitus/

Meta title: What Are the First Signs of Tinnitus? | Ontario Hearing Center

![Man holding ear with What are the first signs of tinnitus text](/assets/img/What-are-the-first-signs-of-Tinnitus-3bb1ea38.webp)

The first sign of tinnitus is usually a sound with no outside source: ringing, buzzing, hissing, or humming in one or both ears. It can come and go or stay constant, and some people first notice it in a quiet room, such as at bedtime.

Ontario Hearing Center is an audiology practice in Brighton. We provide tinnitus evaluation and management, and we have served the Rochester area since 1956.

## What tinnitus sounds like at first

Ringing is the most common description, and tinnitus also sounds like buzzing, roaring, clicking, hissing, humming, or whooshing. Some people hear a pulse that keeps time with their heartbeat. The pitch runs from a low roar to a high squeal, and you can hear it in one ear or both. Other people usually cannot hear it.

## How long the first signs last

Tinnitus can be constant or come and go. Loud sounds can also cause short-term ringing and permanent hearing loss. If the ringing does not fade, or keeps coming back, get it checked.

## Hearing changes that come with early tinnitus

Tinnitus often comes with hearing loss. About 9 in 10 people with tinnitus have some hearing loss, and a hearing test shows whether you do. Some people also notice a feeling of fullness in the ear.

## Effects on sleep, focus, and mood

Tinnitus can make it harder to sleep and focus, and it can affect memory and mood. Quiet rooms make it easier to notice, and stress and anxiety make it feel louder.

## Who is more likely to get tinnitus?

Common risk factors are loud noise exposure and age. Men are more likely to have tinnitus. Smoking, drinking alcohol, high blood pressure and other heart and blood vessel conditions, and a history of head injury also raise the risk.

Some medicines, including high doses of aspirin and some antibiotics, can cause it. Earwax or another blockage in the ear canal can also cause it.

## When to get checked right away

See a doctor right away if tinnitus comes with sudden hearing loss, dizziness, or numbness or weakness in the face, arm, or leg. Get a prompt evaluation for any of these:

- Ringing in one ear only
- A sound that pulses with your heartbeat
- Ringing after a head injury or accident
- Ringing that follows a cold and has not improved in a week
- Anxiety, depression, or lost sleep from tinnitus

Ringing in one ear with a drop in hearing can be sudden sensorineural hearing loss, and treatment can be time sensitive. Audiologists and ENT doctors work together on tinnitus.

## What happens at a tinnitus evaluation?

A tinnitus evaluation starts with a comprehensive hearing test and questions about your tinnitus: when you notice it, what it sounds like, how loud it is, what makes it worse, and whether it affects your sleep or concentration. The [2026 clinical guideline](https://www.newswise.com/articles/living-with-ringing-in-your-ears-updated-guideline-supports-a-personalized-evidence-based-approach-to-tinnitus) from the American Academy of Otolaryngology-Head and Neck Surgery Foundation says every patient with tinnitus should have a comprehensive hearing evaluation.

A doctor will also ask about your history and examine your ears, head, and neck. Scans are limited to people with specific concerning features. Turn down headphone volume and wear hearing protection around loud equipment. A fan, a white noise machine, or soft music makes tinnitus less noticeable. ## Frequently asked questions

### What does tinnitus sound like at first?

Usually ringing, buzzing, hissing, or humming that only you hear. It can be soft or loud, high or low, and constant or on and off.

### How can I tell if I have early tinnitus?

If you hear a sound that no one else hears and nothing in the room is making it, it is likely tinnitus. A tinnitus evaluation finds out what is behind it.

### What are the common early symptoms of tinnitus?

A sound with no outside source is the main one. Trouble sleeping or focusing, and a feeling of fullness in the ear, can come with it.

### Is ringing in the ears a sign of tinnitus?

Yes. Ringing with no outside source is the most common way people describe tinnitus. Earwax or an ear infection can cause it, so have your ears checked.

### Can tinnitus start suddenly?

Yes. Sudden tinnitus, especially in one ear or with a drop in hearing, needs prompt attention from a doctor and a hearing professional.
