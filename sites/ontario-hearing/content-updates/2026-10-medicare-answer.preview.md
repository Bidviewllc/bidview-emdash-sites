## Added

## Does Medicare cover hearing aids in New York?

Original Medicare (Part A and Part B) does not pay for hearing aids or for the exams used to fit them, in New York or any other state.

Medicare Advantage plans (Part C) may include a hearing benefit, and the individual plan sets what that benefit includes, not Medicare.

You don’t have to cover the full cost upfront. Our audiologists at Ontario Hearing Center can guide you through your options, including financing.
