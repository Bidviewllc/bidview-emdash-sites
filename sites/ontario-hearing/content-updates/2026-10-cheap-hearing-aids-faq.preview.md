## Frequently asked questions

### Are cheap hearing aids ever a reasonable option?

For some people with mild, uncomplicated hearing loss, a basic device can provide some benefit. The only way to know is to start with a [hearing test](/hearing-test/). An audiologist can identify your specific type of hearing loss. They can tell you directly if a low-cost device is enough, or if it will leave you with unclear speech and louder background noise.

### Why do audiologist-fitted hearing aids cost more?

The price reflects more than the device. Your audiologist programs your hearing aids to your own hearing test results, verifies them with real-ear measurement to confirm they're delivering the right sound in your ears, and schedules follow-up visits to fine-tune the settings as you adjust. A cheap hearing aid comes with none of that.

### Is financing available for hearing aids in Rochester?

Yes. Ontario Hearing Center partners with CareCredit, which lets you spread the cost of your hearing aids over time. You can find general guidance directly on our [insurance page](/insurance/).

### Does insurance cover hearing aids?

It depends on your plan. Some insurers, like Empire, offer a hearing aid benefit, and individual employer plans may cover hearing aids too, while others do not. Because these insurance benefits can be confusing, the best way to verify your coverage is to contact our office directly. Our team can help you check and explain your available benefits.
