## Starkey Omega AI+

Starkey Omega AI+ is Starkey’s newest premium hearing aid family, released in fall 2026 as the successor to Omega AI. It runs on the new G4 Gen AI Neuro Processor, which uses a brain-inspired design with several deep neural networks (DNNs) to process sound quickly and naturally while using very little power. It’s built for people who want clear speech in any setting along with a long list of everyday smart features.

Omega AI+ comes in rechargeable receiver-in-canal (RIC) styles and custom in-ear models, including a new line of tiny custom non-wireless devices. It offers up to 51 hours of battery life, a waterproof design, and the new StarLink Charger 3.0. Through the My Starkey app, wearers also get health and convenience features.

**Unique Perks and Features:**

- **G4 Gen AI Neuro Processor:** A brain-inspired, multi-DNN chip that processes sound faster and more naturally while saving battery power.
- **DNN Dual Compressor:** A balance of fast compression for clearer speech and slow compression for richer sound quality.
- **Always-on Edge Mode:** Starkey’s AI feature for speech clarity and noise control can now stay on by default, so wearers get the benefit without pressing a button.
- **Sleep Quality Tracking:** RIC wearers get a nightly sleep score with insights on sleep cycles and body position through the My Starkey app.
- **Hearing Aids Left Behind alerts:** The app notifies wearers if they walk away without their hearing aids.
- **All-day rechargeable power:** Up to 51 hours of battery life per charge, paired with the redesigned StarLink Charger 3.0.

## Widex Allure AI

The Widex Allure AI RIC is Widex’s first AI-powered hearing aid. It builds on Widex’s natural sound with a dedicated AI co-processor that the wearer switches on when they want extra help in noisy places. Everyday listening stays natural and clear, and AI support is there on demand when conversations get harder to follow.

The Allure AI RIC is a rechargeable receiver-in-canal (RIC) hearing aid in the same modern design as the standard Allure RIC, available in 10 colors. It offers up to 32 hours of battery life and connects to iPhone and Android devices.

**Unique Perks and Features:**

- **Clarity Boost with AI co-processor:** A dedicated AI chip that the wearer activates in noisy environments for clearer conversations while staying aware of surrounding sounds.
- **Natural sound foundation:** The W1 chip and Precision Hearing Technology deliver Widex’s signature natural sound for everyday listening.
- **Proven speech clarity:** Up to 26% better speech intelligibility for moderate-to-severe hearing loss, with 97% of wearers describing the sound as natural and clear.
- **All-day rechargeable power:** Up to 32 hours of use per charge, including 6 hours of AI use or streaming.
- **Flexible connectivity:** Stream calls and audio through mFi, ASHA, and LE Audio, with telecoil and hands-free calling built in.
- **Charging options for every lifestyle:** Choose from standard, Charge & Clean, or portable Qi-enabled wireless charging.
