### Unitron Moxi S-RX

The Unitron Moxi S-RX was released in July 2026 and is Unitron's first hearing aid with a dedicated AI chip, DEEPSONIC™, built to separate speech from background noise in real time. It's designed for people who struggle most in busy restaurants, crowded rooms, and group conversations.

The Moxi S-RX is a rechargeable receiver-in-canal (RIC) hearing aid with all-day battery life and universal Bluetooth connectivity. It comes in two technology levels, 9 (premium) and 7 (advanced).

For people who rarely face noisy, multi-speaker settings, the standard Moxi S-R offers the Smile platform's core benefits at a lower cost.

**Unique Perks and Features:**

- **SoundSonic360:** AI-powered processing identifies and prioritizes speech from every direction, keeping conversations clear in noisy environments.
- **DEEPSONIC™ AI chip:** A dedicated deep neural network chip handles speech-in-noise separation in real time, rather than relying on software alone.
- **Integra OS automation:** Automatically adjusts to changing listening environments, from quiet rooms to loud crowds, with no manual program changes.
- **AutoFocus 360 (Level 9):** Uses all four microphones across both hearing aids to find and enhance speech coming from any direction.
- **Universal Bluetooth streaming:** Stream phone calls, music, and audio directly from smartphones and other devices, with hands-free calling.
- **Rechargeable convenience:** A built-in rechargeable battery delivers reliable all-day power without battery changes.
