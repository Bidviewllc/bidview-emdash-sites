Component 12 · on the Home hero

Trust bar

Frosted bar along the bottom of the Home hero. The rating panel stays put; brand logos drift slowly behind it and blur as they pass. Hover, keyboard focus or the pause button stops the drift; reduce-motion users get a still row. Press Play to try it. The Home hero layout itself gets finalised with Page 1.
