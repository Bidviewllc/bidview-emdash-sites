Current Phonak hearing aid models

Liberty Hearing Center is proud to be an authorized provider of Phonak hearing aids in College Station, TX. Below is the current lineup we fit, from the flagship Infinio platform to pediatric and single-sided hearing solutions.

Image: Audéo Infinio Ultra Sphere

Phonak Audéo Infinio Ultra Sphere

The Phonak Audéo Infinio Ultra Sphere is Phonak's current flagship. What sets the Ultra Sphere apart is the DEEPSONIC™ chip — the world's first dedicated AI processor built specifically for real-time speech separation.

The DEEPSONIC chip isolates voices from background noise, in real time, from any direction. The result is a level of speech clarity in noisy environments — restaurants, parties, meetings, crowded rooms — that users can actually hear in daily life, not just in a lab.

This is recommended for patients whose primary challenge is following speech in noisy environments such as restaurants, social gatherings, open offices, and family gatherings with multiple speakers.

Unique features
  • 30% better battery efficiency — up to 56 hours of total use, with at least 10 hours in active Spheric Speech Clarity AI mode
  • EasyGuard™ wax management — a sealed, wipe-clean dome system that reduces wax-related maintenance and, according to Phonak, can cut service appointments related to wax buildup by up to 38%
  • One-step Bluetooth pairing — simplified connection to smartphones and other devices
Image: Audéo Infinio Ultra R

Phonak Audéo Infinio Ultra R

The Audéo Infinio Ultra R is a highly capable, well-rounded hearing aid offering strong performance across a wide range of listening environments, a comfortable and discreet RIC form factor, and access to all the connectivity and personalization features of the myPhonak app.

Unique features
  • Battery: Rechargeable — up to 30 hours
  • Connectivity: Universal Bluetooth — pair up to 8 devices, 2 simultaneously
Image: Virto R Infinio

Phonak Virto R Infinio

The Phonak Virto R Infinio is the smallest rechargeable in-the-ear hearing aid Phonak has ever made.

Each Virto R Infinio is individually molded to the shape of the wearer's ear using Phonak's RightFit™ customization process, which incorporates biometric calibration across 1,600 data points to achieve a fit that is as small as possible while optimizing both comfort and sound quality. The result is a hearing aid that sits entirely within the ear, stays securely in place during active use, and is barely visible from the outside.

Unique features
  • Snap and Charge system — no fiddly ports or alignment required — and reaches a full charge in three hours
  • Battery: Rechargeable — up to 30 hours in calm environments; at least 16 hours with 8 hours of streaming
  • Rating: IP68 (dust and moisture resistant)
  • Connectivity: Universal Bluetooth — pair up to 8 devices, 2 simultaneously
Image: Audéo Infinio

Phonak Audéo Infinio

The Phonak Audéo Infinio is a full-featured hearing aid that is CROS-compatible, making it an option for patients with single-sided hearing loss when paired with a Phonak CROS device.

Unique features
  • Battery: Rechargeable options available
  • Connectivity: Universal Bluetooth; CROS compatible
  • Fit: Sits discreetly behind the ear
Image: CROS Infinio

Phonak CROS Infinio

The Phonak CROS Infinio is designed for patients with single-sided deafness or unaidable hearing loss in one ear. A CROS (Contralateral Routing of Signal) device is worn on the non-hearing ear and wirelessly transmits the sounds coming from that side to a compatible Phonak hearing aid worn on the better-hearing ear — giving the listener access to sound from both sides without turning their head or missing speech coming from their weaker side.

Unique features
  • Rechargeable battery
  • Universal Bluetooth connectivity
  • Seamless integration with the myPhonak app
Image: Audéo Fit

Phonak Audéo Fit™

The Phonak Audéo Fit takes the core Phonak hearing aid experience and adds health tracking built directly into the device. Through the myPhonak app, Audéo Fit monitors steps, distance, activity levels, average daily wearing time, and heart rate.

Daily, weekly, monthly, and yearly heart rate overviews are available in the app, giving patients a window into their overall health alongside their hearing.

Unique features
  • Tap Control: a double-tap on the ear answers calls, controls streaming, or activates a voice assistant
  • Battery: Rechargeable
  • Connectivity: Universal Bluetooth
Image: Audéo Lumity

Phonak Audéo™ Lumity

The Phonak Audéo Lumity is the second generation of the world's first waterproof rechargeable hearing aid, carrying an IP68 dust and moisture resistance rating that makes it a strong choice for active patients. It fits mild to profound hearing losses and is available in rechargeable form.

Image: Phonak Slim

Phonak Slim

The Phonak Slim offers the same core technology as the Audéo Lumity in a redesigned form factor built specifically for comfort and a low profile. Its ergonomic shape nestles naturally behind the ear, including for patients who wear glasses, without the visual footprint of a traditional behind-the-ear device.

Unique features
  • Best for: patients who want a discreet, comfortable behind-the-ear hearing aid that works well with glasses
  • Hearing loss range: Mild to severe
  • Battery: Rechargeable
  • Style: Receiver-in-canal (RIC)
Image: Naída Lumity

Phonak Naída Lumity

The Phonak Naída Lumity is Phonak's most powerful hearing solution, built for patients with significant or profound hearing loss who need robust amplification alongside modern features. The Naída platform analyzes 700 environmental scans per second to deliver high-fidelity sound at power levels other hearing aids cannot reach.

Unique features
  • Best for: patients with severe to profound hearing loss who need maximum amplification
  • Battery: Rechargeable; size 675 disposable available in UP model
  • Style: Behind-the-ear (BTE)
Image: Sky Lumity

Phonak Sky Lumity

The Phonak Sky Lumity is designed specifically for children, with SmartSpeech™ Technology that minimizes background noise and prioritizes speech understanding in the dynamic listening environments kids navigate every day — classrooms, playgrounds, sports, and home.

The myPhonak Junior app is the only dedicated pediatric hearing aid app in the industry, giving children age-appropriate control over their hearing while giving parents visibility into wearing time and device performance.

Unique features
  • Best for: children with mild to profound hearing loss
  • Hearing loss range: Mild to profound
  • Battery: Rechargeable; size 675 disposable available in UP model

  • App: myPhonak Junior app

Phonak accessories & assistive technology

Phonak's accessory ecosystem extends the reach of hearing aids into specific listening situations where the devices alone may not fully close the gap.

Roger On™

A wireless microphone that clips to clothing or sits on a table, transmitting the speaker's voice directly to the hearing aid. Roger On automatically detects whether speech is coming from the left or right, preserving spatial awareness in noisy environments.

Roger Select™

A table microphone that automatically identifies and switches between multiple speakers in group conversations — useful in meetings, family dinners, and any situation with several people talking around a table.

TV Connector

Streams television audio directly to compatible hearing aids with no delay, at a volume the hearing aid wearer controls independently of the main speakers.

PartnerMic

A small clip-on microphone worn by a communication partner that streams their voice directly to the hearing aid wearer — ideal for one-on-one conversations in noisy places or over longer distances.

myPhonak App

Available for iPhone and Android; allows volume adjustment, program changes, sound personalization, and remote consultations with a hearing care professional through the Remote Help feature.

Serenity Choice™

High-end custom hearing protection that reduces loud sounds while maintaining speech audibility — a natural fit for musicians, industrial workers, and anyone seeking professional-grade noise protection.

Frequently asked questions about Phonak hearing aids

+What is the difference between the Phonak Infinio Ultra Sphere and the Infinio Ultra R?

The biggest difference comes down to how well each hearing aid handles noisy places. The Infinio Ultra Sphere has a special built-in computer chip — the DEEPSONIC™ chip — that does one job and does it really well: it picks out voices from all the background noise around you. Think of it like noise-canceling headphones, but for conversations.

The Infinio Ultra R is a great all-around hearing aid that works well in most situations, but it does not have that same dedicated chip. If your biggest struggle is following conversations in loud restaurants, busy rooms, or noisy family gatherings, the Ultra Sphere is the stronger choice. If you want a reliable hearing aid for everyday listening, the Ultra R gets the job done beautifully.

+What is the battery life of Phonak rechargeable hearing aids?

How long the battery lasts depends on which model you have and how you use it. The Infinio Ultra Sphere can last up to 56 hours total, though that drops to around 10 hours if you are using it hard in loud, busy places all day. The Infinio Ultra R and the Virto R Infinio both last up to 30 hours in everyday listening situations. All rechargeable Phonak hearing aids charge while you sleep, so they are ready to go every morning, just like plugging in your phone at night.

+How do I know which Phonak hearing aid is right for me?

The best hearing aid for you depends on how well you hear right now, which situations give you the most trouble, how you spend your days, and what fits your budget. Someone who struggles to follow conversations in noisy places will have different needs than someone who mostly listens in quiet settings at home.

At Liberty Hearing Center, we start with a hearing test and a conversation about your life before we ever suggest a specific hearing aid. We understand that each hearing loss is unique, and the best hearing aid for a person is one chosen by someone who truly knows your hearing.
