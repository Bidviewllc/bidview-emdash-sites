Before you come in

Answers to what patients ask most

How long is a first visit?

Plan on about an hour. That covers your hearing history, a full evaluation, and time to talk through the results without rushing.

What should I bring?

Your insurance card, a list of current medications, and any hearing aids you already own — even the ones sitting in a drawer.

Do you file insurance?

We work with most major plans and VA authorizations, and we'll verify your benefits before your appointment so there are no surprises.

Prefer to talk it through?

Call us and we'll find a time that works.

One short call is often all it takes to know where to start. We're happy to answer questions before you commit to anything.
