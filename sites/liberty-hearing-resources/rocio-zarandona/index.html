Our Team

Rocio Zarandona

Practice Manager & Co-Founder | Liberty Hearing Center | College Station, TX

Book Your Appointment Call 979-450-7996

Rocio is the first person most patients meet at Liberty Hearing Center - by phone or in person.

Born and raised in Spain, Rocio relocated to the United States after marrying Dr. Chris Duhon. She brings with her a warmth and genuine care for people that makes every patient interaction feel less like a transaction and more like a conversation with someone who is actually glad you called.

Fluent in both English and Spanish, Rocio has a particular gift for making patients from all backgrounds feel comfortable, understood, and supported, whether they are scheduling their first appointment, navigating an insurance question, or just trying to figure out what comes next.

She takes the complexity out of the administrative side of hearing care so patients can focus on the part that actually matters: getting better hearing.

Rota, Spain

Her Background

Rocio comes to Liberty Hearing Center with real healthcare experience behind her. She served as an Executive Administrative Assistant and Medical Translator and Interpreter at the U.S. Naval Hospital in Rota, Spain, working alongside physicians and healthcare professionals to support patients and ensure clear, accurate communication across language barriers.

That experience sharpened everything she brings to her role today: strong organizational skills, attention to detail, the ability to navigate complex systems on a patient's behalf, and a deep understanding of how much the non-clinical side of a healthcare visit matters to the people going through it.

When the administrative experience is handled well - when insurance is verified before the appointment, when questions get real answers, when no one is left wondering what to do next — patients arrive ready to focus on their hearing. Rocio makes that happen.

What Patients Can Expect

From the first phone call to the last follow-up, Rocio is committed to making every interaction with Liberty Hearing Center feel easy and welcoming. Patients can expect:

  • A warm, unhurried welcome
  • Clear guidance on scheduling, insurance, and what to bring to their appointment
  • Straightforward answers to administrative questions without the runaround
  • A familiar, friendly face that remembers who they are

At Liberty Hearing Center, no one gets lost in the shuffle. Rocio makes sure of that.

A Little More About Rocio

Outside the office, family comes first. Rocio believes deeply in community and in giving back to the people around her.

She is also the reason Liberty Hearing Center runs as smoothly as it does. Ask anyone on the team.

Independent, patient-centered hearing care for Central Texas.
