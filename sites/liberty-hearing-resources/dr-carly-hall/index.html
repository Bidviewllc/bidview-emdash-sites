Dr. Carly Hall, AuD

Doctor of Audiology | Pediatrics, Cochlear Implants & Tinnitus | College Station, TX

Schedule With Dr. Hall Call 979-450-7996

Dr. Carly Hall is a Doctor of Audiology with a straightforward approach to patient care: take the time to understand who is in front of you before making any recommendation.

She grew up in Thibodaux, Louisiana, a close-knit community where genuine care for neighbors is a way of life. That upbringing shows up in every appointment she has.

She brings clinical depth and genuine warmth, whether she is working with a newborn who needs a follow-up hearing screening, a child navigating their first cochlear implant mapping, or an adult who has been quietly struggling with tinnitus for years and finally decided to do something about it.

Dr. Hall chose audiology for a reason that has stayed with her from the beginning: helping people hear what they have been missing and feel connected to the world around them again.

5+ years of clinical experience

Clinical Interests & Experience

Dr. Hall has more than five years of combined graduate and professional clinical experience, with focused depth in three areas she is particularly passionate about:

Pediatric Audiology

Children are Dr. Hall's specialty, and the work comes naturally to her. She evaluates hearing in patients from birth through school age, using OAE technology for newborn follow-up screenings, Visual Reinforcement Audiometry (VRA) for infants as young as 4 to 6 months, and ABR/ASSR testing for children who need objective measures. She has the patience young patients require and the clinical skill to get accurate results even with the most reluctant little ones in the sound booth.

Cochlear Implants

Dr. Hall brings three years of dedicated cochlear implant experience to Liberty Hearing Center, programming, mapping, and supporting patients through the ongoing process of getting the most out of their devices. Cochlear implant care is not a one-time appointment; it is a long-term relationship between a patient and their audiologist, and Dr. Hall approaches it that way. Liberty Hearing Center also provides cochlear implant mapping and follow-up care for patients implanted by Brazos Valley ENTs and otologists.

Tinnitus Management

Tinnitus is one of the most undertreated conditions in audiology, partly because it is invisible and partly because patients are often told there is nothing to be done. Dr. Hall takes a different approach. She evaluates tinnitus thoroughly, assesses how it is affecting each patient's daily life, and builds individualized management plans from sound therapy, counseling-based approaches, and hearing aid technology when appropriate.

When she is not in the clinic, Dr. Hall can usually be found in her garden or working on a painting. She is a fellow Louisianan, which, alongside Dr. Duhon, means Liberty Hearing Center has more than its share of people who understand good food, warm hospitality, and the deep pull of the Gulf South.
