Dr. Chris Duhon, AuD

Doctor of Audiology | Founder, Liberty Hearing Center | College Station, TX

Schedule With Dr. Duhon Call 979-450-7996

Dr. Chris Duhon grew up between two worlds - born in Charleston, West Virginia, and raised in the heart of southwest Louisiana, where Cajun culture, good food, and a deep appreciation for family shaped who he is.

He has carried both of those upbringings with him ever since: the warmth of the South and the joie de vivre of Louisiana run through everything he does, including the way he practices audiology.

After earning his undergraduate degree in Communication Disorders from the University of Louisiana Lafayette in 1999 and his master's in Audiology from LSU Health Sciences Center in New Orleans in 2003, Dr. Duhon joined the United States Navy - a decision that would take him around the world and define the next two decades of his life.

He retired in 2025 as a Lieutenant Commander after 20 years of service, bringing his family to College Station, a place that already felt like home, thanks to family ties to Aggieland, a love of the Texas Hill Country, red-dirt music and the pull of a community worth putting down roots in.

Liberty Hearing Center is the practice he always wanted to build: a place where patients are treated like neighbors, professionals are given the space to do their best work, and everyone leaves better connected than when they walked in.

2005 – 2025

Military Service

Dr. Duhon served the United States Navy from 2005 to 2025, working as an audiologist and healthcare leader across a wide range of clinical settings - from military hospitals and specialty clinics to treatment facilities in the U.S. and abroad.

Throughout his career, he cared for active-duty service members, veterans, and military families - conducting diagnostic hearing and balance evaluations, fitting hearing aids, and helping patients maintain the ability to communicate and stay connected to the people around them.

His assignments took him to Pensacola, Florida; Rota, Spain; San Diego, California; Parris Island, South Carolina; and Norfolk, Virginia - each post shaping his clinical perspective and his commitment to serving diverse patient populations.

Dr. Duhon's work extended beyond individual patient care. He supported Department of Defense schools, helping identify children with hearing concerns early so they had the best possible foundation for learning.

He also collaborated with leading researchers in hearing conservation, contributing to advances in hearing protection device fit-testing and noise-induced hearing loss prevention for service members working in high-noise environments.

His military service gave him a firsthand understanding of how hearing loss affects not just communication, but confidence, safety, and quality of life, for service members and their families alike.

That understanding is the foundation of every clinical decision he makes at Liberty Hearing Center.

Areas of Focus

Dr. Duhon has been practicing audiology since 2003.

Those 23 years of clinical work span military medicine, school-based hearing health, hearing conservation research, and private practice.

His areas of particular depth include:
  • Adult Hearing Evaluations
  • Veteran C&P Hearing Evaluations
  • Hearing Conservation Counseling
  • School-Based Hearing Screenings
  • Hearing Aid Fittings with Real-Ear Verification
  • Pediatric Hearing Evaluation

A Little More About Dr. Duhon

He is one of six brothers. He met his best friend — and future wife, Rocio (pronounced Ro-Thee-oh)— while stationed in Rota, Spain, and the two of them have raised two children through a life that has spanned continents. When he is not in the clinic, you will likely find him on the water — boating, fishing, sailing, or swimming — or listening to live music or catching up with family on the weekends.

His love of Red Dirt music, Shiner Bock, and everything Aggieland made College Station an easy final destination after a career that took him around the world.

He is glad to be here. So is his family. And if you ask him, that is exactly how a good story ends.

Why He Built Liberty Hearing Center

Dr. Duhon founded Liberty Hearing Center to fulfill a goal he had been working toward his entire career: to lead a practice built around two things at once: exceptional patient care and a workplace where the people providing that care actually thrive.

He wanted to create a place where patients with hearing loss could regain the ability to hear, communicate, and connect with the people they love. Simultaneously, he wanted to build a practice that did not burn out the professionals doing the work, because he believes that a team with time for their own families and their own lives shows up better for their patients.

The result is Liberty Hearing Center: a locally owned audiology practice in College Station where everyone who walks through the door is treated like a neighbor, and everyone who works there is treated like family.

Independent, patient-centered hearing care for Central Texas.
