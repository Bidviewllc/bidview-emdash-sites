Unbundled Service Fees

Evaluation of Outer Ear System

Checking the ear canal itself, because a physical blockage is one of the simplest causes of hearing trouble and one of the fastest to fix.

Otoscopic Exam
Included

During an otoscopic exam, we look inside the ear canal with a lighted scope. It confirms the canal is clear and the eardrum is intact before any testing begins, and rules out wax, inflammation, or injury as the reason sound is not getting through. This is included at no charge for every patient.

Evaluation of Middle Ear System

Testing the eardrum and the small bones behind it, where a cause of hearing loss is treatable and sometimes temporary.

Tympanometry and, if required, Acoustic Reflex Testing
$50–100

Tympanometry measures how well your eardrum moves when air pressure changes, which identifies fluid behind the eardrum, Eustachian tube dysfunction, a perforation, or a problem with the middle ear bones. Acoustic reflex testing adds a second layer of information about the hearing nerve and the brainstem pathway, and helps distinguish between types of hearing loss.

Both tests take a few minutes and involve nothing more than a soft probe at the entrance of the ear canal and a gentle puff of air. The price range reflects whether acoustic reflex testing is needed in addition to tympanometry.

Tympanometry alone requires a physician's referral; without one, it must be bundled with a comprehensive hearing evaluation, since insurance won't reimburse tympanometry as a standalone service.

Earwax Removal (Cerumen Removal)
$125

Excess wax is one of the most common and most easily resolved causes of muffled hearing, a plugged or full sensation, and hearing aid whistling. Removal is performed in the office using clinical instruments, after the canal and eardrum have been inspected to determine the safest method for your ears.

This is not a service to attempt at home. Cotton swabs and over-the-counter kits push wax deeper against the eardrum more reliably than they remove it.

Evaluation of Inner Ear System

Measuring how much hearing loss is present, in which ear, at which pitches, and where in the auditory system it originates.

Visual Reinforcement Audiometry (ages 3 months to 2.5 years)
$250

A behavioral hearing test built for babies and toddlers. Sound is paired with a lighted or animated toy that rewards a head turn toward the speaker, which lets an audiologist map hearing levels in a child far too young to raise a hand or answer a question. Testing is done at a pace set by the child.

Before we can evaluate the inner ear, we must first rule out any issues with the middle ear. This requires a tympanometry test that is included in the pricing below.

Conditioned Play Audiometry (ages 3 to 5)
$250

Hearing testing run as a game. The child learns to drop a block in a bucket or stack a ring each time a tone comes through the headphones, which holds a preschooler's attention long enough to get reliable, ear-by-ear results across the frequency range.

Before we can evaluate the inner ear, we must first rule out any issues with the middle ear. This requires a tympanometry test that is included in the pricing below.

Comprehensive Hearing Evaluation (age 5 through adult)
$250

The full diagnostic evaluation. It includes a detailed case history, pure tone testing across the frequency range, bone conduction testing to identify where the hearing loss originates, speech testing to measure how clearly you understand words rather than just detect tones, and a plain-language review of your results and what they mean for your daily communication.

Speech-in-noise testing is added when indicated, because a busy restaurant or a group conversation is where most people notice a problem first.

Before we can evaluate the inner ear, we must first rule out any issues with the middle ear. This requires a tympanometry test that is included in the pricing below.

Cochlear Implant Initial Programming
$300

The first activation of a cochlear implant after surgery. Each electrode is individually set to the levels appropriate for your hearing, establishing the map the device will use to convert sound into electrical signals. This appointment is longer and more involved than a follow-up because every parameter is being set from the beginning.

Cochlear Implant Subsequent Programming
$200

Follow-up mapping visits that refine those settings as your auditory system adapts to the implant. Ongoing adjustment is a normal part of cochlear implant care and directly affects how well the device performs in real listening situations.

Tinnitus Assessment
$100

A standard hearing test measures which tones you can detect. A tinnitus assessment is different — it's a structured evaluation of your tinnitus itself: what it sounds like, how loud or bothersome it feels, when it's worse, how much it masks other sounds, and how it's affecting your sleep, concentration, and quality of life.

Tinnitus is subjective, so there's no instrument that measures it directly the same way that an audiometer measures hearing. Instead, we rely on your description, validated questionnaires, and case history to understand the full picture — then recommend in-house counseling and management strategies, or a referral for higher-level evaluation, depending on severity.

Those findings shape your management plan: sound therapy, counseling-based approaches, hearing aids with tinnitus features, or a combination.

Tinnitus assessment can be purchased alone if the patient has had a hearing evaluation within the past year (either with us or another provider). If not, a comprehensive hearing evaluation ($200) and tympanometry ($50) are required alongside the tinnitus assessment ($100), for a minimum total of $350.

Liberty Hearing Center provides cochlear implant mapping and follow-up care for patients implanted by Brazos Valley ENTs and otologists.

Hearing Aid Services

These services are included at no additional cost for three years when you purchase hearing aids through Liberty Hearing Center on a bundled plan. They are available individually to unbundled patients, and to patients who purchased hearing aids elsewhere.

Redux Hearing Aid Drying
$50

Moisture is one of the leading causes of hearing aid failure, and it accumulates from sweat, humidity, and condensation long before the device stops working outright. The Redux system uses a vacuum-based process to draw moisture out of the internal components in a single in-office visit and reports how much it pulled out.

Patients who live through Brazos Valley summers, work outdoors, or exercise in their devices benefit from this on a regular schedule rather than waiting for a failure.

Redux, Hearing Aid Clean, Check & Programming Adjustment
$150

All three services in one appointment: moisture removal, a full cleaning and performance check, and a programming adjustment.

Hearing Aid Clean & Check
$50

A professional cleaning of the receivers, microphone ports, wax guards, and tubing. Daily home cleaning helps, but wax and debris reach places a brush cannot, and a device that has quietly drifted out of spec sounds like worsening hearing to the person wearing it.

Best treated as routine maintenance on a set schedule, not something to book only after the sound gets bad.

Real Ear Verification
$100

A small probe microphone placed in your ear canal measures what the hearing aid is actually delivering to your eardrum using real speech signals. Without it, programming relies on manufacturer averages that account for nothing about the dimensions of your own ear canal. Two patients with identical audiograms and identical devices get different results from ear anatomy alone.

Real-ear verification is the standard of care recommended by the American Academy of Audiology and the American Speech-Language-Hearing Association, and research links verified fittings to better word recognition and higher patient satisfaction.

Bluetooth Troubleshooting
$50

A dedicated appointment to get your phone and your hearing aids working together properly: Bluetooth pairing, the manufacturer's app, streaming calls and audio, hands-free settings, and the volume and program controls on your phone.

Worth booking with a new set of devices, and again any time you switch phones — a new handset can break a pairing that worked fine for years.

Why prices vary within each tier

Liberty Hearing Center partners with seven leading hearing aid manufacturers, giving you access to a wide range of trusted technology. Because manufacturer costs differ, prices within each tier reflect the specific brand and device that best fits your hearing needs, lifestyle, and budget. During your consultation, we'll help you find the right option — and the right price point — within your tier.

Premium Hearing Aid

$3,970–$4,650 unbundled / $6,670–$7,350 bundled

The top technology level from each manufacturer we carry. Built for the hardest listening situations there are: a crowded restaurant, a room full of overlapping conversation, a reverberant church or auditorium. These devices carry the most advanced speech-in-noise processing, automatic directionality, and connectivity features available, and they make the most decisions on your behalf as your environment changes.

The right choice for patients whose daily life regularly puts them in noise they cannot control.

Active Lifestyle

$3,100–$3,500 unbundled / $5,800–$6,200 bundled

Strong performance in noise and full wireless connectivity, for patients who are out in the world consistently — work meetings, restaurants, group activities, travel, worship services. The processing handles demanding environments well, with fewer of the automatic refinements that the premium level applies in the most complex settings.

Casual Listener

$2,660–$2,800 unbundled / $5,360–$5,500 bundled

Suited to environments that are largely predictable: home, television, one-on-one and small-group conversation, quieter social settings. Background noise is managed capably without the processing power built for a packed restaurant.

A sound fit for patients whose listening demands are steady and whose difficult situations are occasional rather than daily.

Essential Listening

$2,000 unbundled / $4,950 bundled

Entry-level amplification covering the fundamentals: clear speech in quiet, conversation with one person, television at a reasonable volume. It is a genuine hearing aid fitted and verified to the same clinical standard as every other tier, at the lowest price point we can offer.

The starting point for patients working within a tight budget or with straightforward listening needs, and a real improvement over untreated hearing loss.

Bundled vs. unbundled

Bundled plans fold three years of hearing aid services into the price of the devices. Unbundled pricing lowers the up-front cost and charges for each visit as you use it.
