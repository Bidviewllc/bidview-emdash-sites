Phonak Hearing Aids

Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers is an authorized provider of Phonak hearing aids in Hilton Head, SC & Bluffton, SC.

Phonak believes that “Well-Hearing is Well-Being.” This means hearing is more than just sound—it helps with thinking, feeling, and staying connected to others. Phonak’s advanced hearing aids make it easier for people of all ages to hear clearly, enjoy conversations, and take part in everyday life.

Current Phonak Hearing Aid Models

Phonak Virto Infinio

Phonak Virto Infinio is a custom in-the-ear (ITE) hearing aid released in 2025, built for those who want powerful performance in a discreet design. Each device is custom-molded to match the shape of your ear, giving you a snug, comfortable fit that lasts all day.

Packed with advanced sound technology, Virto Infinio delivers clear, natural hearing in a small, almost invisible shell, perfect for anyone who wants great sound without drawing attention.

Unique Features:

  • Custom-Made Fit: Each device is individually molded to the shape of your ear, ensuring a secure, comfortable fit that’s easy to wear all day.

  • AutoSense OS 6.0: Automatically adjusts settings based on your surroundings, providing seamless hearing whether you’re in quiet, noisy, or dynamic environments.

  • Durable & Water-Resistant: Rated IP68 for dust and moisture resistance, making it ideal for active lifestyles and daily wear.

Multiple Technology Levels: Available in four tiers (I90, I70, I50, I30) to meet a range of hearing needs and budgets.

Phonak Audéo Sphere

This high-tech hearing aid delivers clear sound and automatically adjusts to different environments. Whether you’re in a quiet room, a noisy restaurant, or enjoying music, the Audéo Sphere ensures the best hearing experience.

Key Features:

  • Rechargeable Battery – Lasts all day on a single charge.
  • Smart Technology – Helps separate speech from background noise.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity – Connects to phones for calls, music, and volume control.

Phonak Audéo Infinio

The Audéo Infinio makes speech clearer in noisy places and reduces listening effort. It automatically adjusts sounds and works easily with smartphones.

Key Features:

  • CROS Compatibility – Designed for those with single-sided hearing loss.
  • Roger Direct Ready – Improves hearing in noisy settings and at a distance.
  • Multiple Bluetooth Connections – Pairs with up to 8 devices and connects to 2 at the same time.

Phonak CROS Infinio

For those with single-sided deafness, this hearing aid transfers sound from the non-hearing side to the hearing ear, making conversations easier to follow.

Key Features:

  • Hands-Free Calls & Streaming – Works at twice the usual distance.
  • Rechargeable Battery – Long-lasting and easy to charge.
  • Universal Bluetooth Connectivity – Pairs with most Bluetooth devices.

Phonak Audéo Fit

Perfect for active people, the Audéo Fit tracks health data like steps, activity levels, and heart rate through the myPhonak app.

Key Features:

  • Health Tracking – Monitors physical activity and well-being.
  • Tap Control – Tap to answer calls, adjust volume, and switch settings.
  • Universal Bluetooth Connectivity – Connects to phones, TVs, and other devices.

Phonak Audéo Lumity

The Audéo Lumity improves speech clarity in noisy places. It uses artificial intelligence (AI) to adjust to different environments, helping you hear better in conversations.

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity – Works with the myPhonak app and other Bluetooth devices.
  • Personalized Settings – Customize your hearing experience.
  • Remote Support – Connect with a hearing specialist virtually.

Phonak Slim

The Phonak Slim has the same great features as the Audéo Lumity but comes in a sleek, comfortable design that fits well behind the ear—even for glasses wearers.

Key Features:

  • Ergonomic Design – A stylish, comfortable fit.
  • Bluetooth & App Control – Adjust settings easily.
  • Rechargeable Battery – Provides all-day hearing support.

Phonak CROS Lumity

For those with single-sided hearing loss, the CROS Lumity makes it easier to hear from all directions by reducing background noise and improving speech clarity.

Key Features:

  • Wireless Sound Transmission – Sends sound from the non-hearing ear to the hearing ear.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity – Connects with the myPhonak app and other Bluetooth devices.
  • Remote Help – Get virtual support from a hearing specialist.

Phonak Naída Lumity

The Naída Lumity is designed for people with severe to profound hearing loss. It scans the environment 700 times per second to provide the best sound quality.

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity – Works with smartphones and other devices.
  • Personalized Settings – Adjust hearing preferences using the myPhonak app.
  • Multiple Power Options – Available in rechargeable or size 675 battery models.

Phonak Sky Lumity

Built for children, the Sky Lumity helps kids hear clearly in different places like classrooms, home, and play areas.

Key Features:

  • SmartSpeech™ Technology – Focuses on speech over background noise.
  • Kid-Friendly App – The myPhonak Junior app allows kids to adjust settings.
  • Tamper-Resistant Design – Safe and secure for young children.

Phonak Virto Paradise

The Virto Paradise is a small, custom-fit hearing aid that sits inside the ear for a more discreet look. It reduces noise and enhances speech clarity.

Key Features:

  • Custom Fit – Designed to match the shape of your ear.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity – Connects to smartphones and TVs.
  • Multiple Battery Options – Available in different sizes.

Phonak Assistive Devices

Phonak also offers extra devices to improve hearing in different situations:

  • Phonak Serenity Choice – Protects ears from loud noise while keeping important sounds clear.
  • Roger On – A microphone that helps with conversations in noisy places or from a distance.
  • Roger Select – A table microphone that picks up voices in group settings.
  • TV Connector – Streams sound from your TV directly to your hearing aids.
  • PartnerMic – A clip-on microphone for one-on-one conversations.
