Widex Hearing Aids

Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers is an authorized provider of Widex hearing aids in Hilton Head, SC & Bluffton, SC.

Widex is one of the top brands bringing artificial intelligence (AI) to hearing aids. Widex hearing aids offer excellent performance, combining human and AI technology. 

Over the years, they have created hearing aids that provide a clear and natural sound. Widex hearing aids also help people with tinnitus (ringing in the ears) to reduce discomfort.

Widex Hearing Aids in Hilton Head, SC & Bluffton, SC

Below are the Widex hearing aids available at Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers.

Widex Allure

The Widex Allure carries the brand’s most advanced hearing aid platform, offering the perfect blend of cutting-edge technology and elegant design. It is crafted to deliver the most natural hearing experience possible, helping you reconnect with the world through clear speech, accurate environmental sound processing, and intuitive adjustments.

 

Unique Perks and Features:

 

  • Powered by the W1 Chip: Widex’s fastest chip delivers quick, clear, and natural sound.
  • Allure PureSound™ with ZeroDelay Technology: Helps you hear speech without distortion or echo, creating a more lifelike experience.
  • Speech Enhancer Pro: Lowers background noise so voices stand out, making conversations easier—even in noisy places.
  • Dynamic Feedback Controller: Adapts to your ear’s shape to reduce annoying whistling or squealing sounds.
  • Smooth Streaming: Enjoy music, podcasts, and calls with steady sound—no sudden volume changes or outside interference.
  • Widex Allure App: Easily adjust settings and personalize your hearing experience right from your smartphone.

    Widex SmartRIC

    Widex SmartRIC is designed to help you hear better in noisy places. Its unique L-shape design sits higher on the ear, making voices clearer. The battery lasts up to 37 hours, so you can use it all day without worry.

    The charger is small and portable, giving you a week’s worth of power so you can stay connected anywhere. The SmartRIC also has an advanced microphone cover that helps reduce background noise, so you hear more clearly.

    Key Features:

    • App: Works with the Moment app, allowing you to adjust settings to fit your needs.
    • Bluetooth: Connects to both iPhone and Android devices for easy streaming.
    • Tinnitus Relief: Uses special Zen sounds to help manage ringing in the ears.

    Widex Moment Sheer RIC

    The Widex Moment Sheer RIC (receiver-in-the-canal) hearing aid has a sleek new design and an improved charger. It allows you to personalize your hearing through the Moment app. The dual AI technology helps you get the best sound possible.

    This hearing aid is great for people with mild to severe hearing loss. It comes in both rechargeable and disposable battery options. The battery lasts about 29 hours without streaming or 16 hours with 8 hours of streaming.

    Key Features:

    • Bluetooth: Connects to both iPhone and Android devices.
    • App: Lets you adjust settings for a perfect fit.
    • Tinnitus Relief: Zen programs provide relaxing sounds to help with tinnitus.

    Widex Moment RIC

    The Widex Moment RIC hearing aid is small and gives a natural sound experience. It processes sound faster, making speech clearer, even in noisy places. Studies show that 91% of users are happy with the sound quality, and many prefer it over their old hearing aids.

    This model works for mild to severe hearing loss and comes with either a rechargeable or disposable battery option.

    Key Features:

    • Bluetooth: Connects to iPhones and Androids for streaming.
    • App: Customize your hearing settings for a better experience.
    • Tinnitus Relief: Zen programs offer special sounds to help with tinnitus.

    Widex Moment BTE

    Widex Moment BTE (behind-the-ear) uses AI to learn what sounds you like. The SoundSense Learn feature adjusts settings automatically based on what you need at the moment.

    This hearing aid fits mild to severe hearing loss and comes with a rechargeable or size 13 disposable battery.

    Key Features:

    • Bluetooth: Works with iPhones and Androids.
    • App: Adjust settings easily based on your needs.
    • Tinnitus Relief: Zen sounds help reduce ringing in the ears.

    Widex Moment CROS

    Widex leads the way in CROS technology, which helps people who can hear in only one ear. The Widex CROS sends sound from the ear that cannot hear to the ear that can. This makes it easier to hear where sounds are coming from and improves listening in noisy places.

    It can be used as a CROS (for hearing loss in one ear) or a BiCROS (for hearing loss in both ears but worse in one). The Widex CROS uses less power than other CROS devices, making it more efficient.

    Widex Moment ITE

    Widex Moment ITE (in-the-ear) hearing aids are custom-made to fit your ear perfectly. They automatically adjust to give clear and natural sound.

    Widex Moment ITE is great for mild to moderate hearing loss. It comes with size 10 or 312 disposable batteries, lasting between 105 and 150 hours, depending on use.

    Key Features:

    • Custom Fit: Made to fit your ear shape perfectly.
    • Connectivity: Works with Bluetooth and remote control apps.
    • Tinnitus Relief: Zen programs help reduce tinnitus symptoms.

    Widex Assistive Listening Devices

    • Widex TV Play: Streams TV sound directly to your hearing aids for clear sound.
    • Widex Sound Assist: Works as a microphone, hands-free device, and remote control.
    • Com-Dex, RC-Dex, TV-Dex: Additional accessories for better hearing control.
