Personalized Hearing Care

Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers is a hearing aid clinic with hearing aid specialists providing hearing tests, tinnitus management, ear wax removal, and professional hearing services in Hilton Head, SC & Bluffton, SC.

Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers is committed to helping you hear your best. With convenient locations in Hilton Head, SC & Bluffton, SC, our team is proud to provide personalized hearing care to individuals and families throughout South Carolina.

We believe better hearing leads to a better life—and we’re here to walk with you every step of the way. Whether you’re just starting to notice changes in your hearing or have been wearing hearing aids for years, we’re here to support your journey with care, compassion, and expertise.

Our licensed hearing instrument specialists offer a full range of services, including hearing evaluations, hearing aid fittings, adjustments, repairs, and ongoing support.

We carry the latest hearing aid technology from top manufacturers, and we’ll work with you to find the right solution for your needs, lifestyle, and budget.
