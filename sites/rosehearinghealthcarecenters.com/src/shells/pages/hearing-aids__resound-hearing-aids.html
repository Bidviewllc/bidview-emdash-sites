Current ReSound Hearing Aid Models at Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers

These are the ReSound hearing aids available at Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers.

ReSound Enzo IA

The ReSound Enzo IA is designed for severe to profound hearing loss. It is the world’s smallest rechargeable Super Power hearing aid and the first of its kind to support Bluetooth LE Audio and Auracast broadcast streaming.

With a stylish, durable design, the ReSound Enzo IA is built for the most challenging listening situations. It offers exceptional clarity, feedback control, and all-day battery life.

Unique Perks and Features:

  • Clear Focus: Automatically reduces background noise while highlighting speech.
  • 360 All-Around listening: Naturally shifts attention between voices and surrounding sounds.
  • Comfortable and weatherproof: Lightweight, all-day wear with protective nanocoating.
  • Flexible connectivity: Supports Bluetooth® LE Audio, Auracast™, and direct streaming from multiple devices.

ReSound Vivia

ReSound Vivia™ is a smart hearing aid that helps you focus on the sounds you want to hear. It uses special technology that works with your brain to make hearing feel natural. Whether you’re in a noisy room or a quiet place, ReSound Vivia helps you stay connected.

Why You’ll Love It:

  • All-Day Power: The battery lasts all day, even when streaming calls or music.
  • Weatherproof: Protected against water and dust.
  • Bluetooth & Auracast™: Connects easily to phones, TVs, and more.
  • Auracast Assistant: Helps you tune into public broadcasts at places like theaters or airports.

ReSound Savi

ReSound Savi™ is a small, comfortable hearing aid that gives you clear sound all day. It’s easy to use and connects to your favorite devices with Bluetooth®.

Why You’ll Love It:

  • All-Day Power: Works all day, even with streaming.
  • Weatherproof: Keeps working in dusty or wet conditions.
  • Bluetooth & Auracast™: Connects to your phone, TV, and other devices.
  • Auracast Assistant: Helps you hear announcements in public places.
  • Hands-Free Calls: Answer calls without holding your phone—just tap and talk!

ReSound Nexia

ReSound Nexia uses Organic Hearing™ to give you the most natural listening experience. You can stream calls and music from iPhones, iPads, and some Android devices directly to your hearing aids. It even works as a headset for Mac computers, making online meetings and video calls easier.

For people with hearing loss in one ear, ReSound Nexia offers a CROS system. This sends sound from the weaker ear to the stronger ear, helping you hear clearly from both sides.

Why You’ll Love It:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Uses the latest Bluetooth for great sound and long battery life.
  • Auracast Broadcast Audio: This lets you stream public broadcasts straight to your hearing aids.
  • App & Remote Help: Use the ReSound Smart 3D app to adjust settings, fine-tune sound, and manage tinnitus.

ReSound Omnia RIE & Mini RIE

ReSound Omnia hearing aids are designed to provide natural sound and a comfortable fit. They come in two styles: RIE (Receiver-in-Ear) and Mini RIE. These hearing aids work well for mild to severe hearing loss and come with rechargeable or battery-powered options.

Why You’ll Love It:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Works with iPhones, iPads, and Android devices for hands-free calls.
  • Fitting Assistance: Check My Fit helps ensure your hearing aids are placed correctly.
  • App & Remote Help: Use the ReSound Smart 3D app to control your hearing aids.
  • Tinnitus Relief: The ReSound Relief app offers sound therapy and relaxation exercises.

ReSound Omnia ITE

ReSound Omnia also comes in In-The-Ear (ITE) styles, designed to fit inside your ear for a custom and comfortable fit. Since they sit inside the ear canal, they help block out wind noise and won’t interfere with glasses.

ReSound Omnia ITE hearing aids come in different sizes to match your needs:

  • In-the-Canal (ITC)
  • Completely-in-the-Canal (CIC)
  • Invisible-in-the-Canal (IIC)

 

These work well for mild to profound hearing loss and come with rechargeable or battery-powered options.

Why You’ll Love It:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Pairs with iPhones, iPads, and Android devices.
  • Fitting Assistance: Check My Fit helps you wear them correctly.
  • App & Remote Help: Adjust your hearing aids from your phone.
  • Tinnitus Relief: Get sound therapy and relaxation exercises with the ReSound Relief app.

ReSound Omnia BTE & Power BTE

The Behind-the-Ear (BTE) style is also available in the Omnia family. These hearing aids are great for people with severe to profound hearing loss. They make speech 150% clearer in noisy places, so you can focus on conversations better.

These hearing aids work well for mild to profound hearing loss and come with rechargeable batteries.

Why You’ll Love It:

Frequently Asked Questions

What are the levels of ReSound technology?

The newest ReSound Omnia hearing aids come in three levels:

  • 9 (highest level, best features)
  • 7 (mid-level)
  • 5 (basic level)

How do I adjust my ReSound hearing aids?

You can adjust your hearing aids with the ReSound Smart 3D app, available for iPhone and Android. This app works with ReSound Omnia, ReSound ONE, and other ReSound models.

How long do ReSound hearing aids last?

Most hearing aids last about 5 years, but this depends on how well you take care of them. Some people upgrade sooner to get the latest features. Your audiologist can help you decide when it’s time for new hearing aids.
