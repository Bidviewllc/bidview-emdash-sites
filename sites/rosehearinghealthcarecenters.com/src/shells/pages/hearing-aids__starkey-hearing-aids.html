Starkey Hearing Aids

Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers is an authorized provider of Starkey hearing aids in Hilton Head, SC & Bluffton, SC.

Starkey is a leader in hearing aid technology featuring advanced and custom-fit hearing aids that are reliable and comfortable.

Starkey also uses artificial intelligence (AI) to make hearing clearer and easier. They offer many different models to fit each person’s unique needs.

Current Starkey Hearing Aid Models

Starkey Signature Series

The Starkey Signature Series gives you amazing sound in hearing aids that are super tiny and almost invisible. They are custom-made to fit inside your ear, so no one can tell you’re wearing them. You’ll hear your friends, family, and the world around you clearly, without anyone knowing why. Whether you want rechargeable, adjustable, or the smallest size possible, there’s a signature style just for you.

Unique Features:

  • Discreet: Nearly invisible and fits deep in your ear.
  • Natural Sound: Clear, lifelike sound so you can enjoy every moment.
  • Rechargeable: Up to 38 hours of power-no tiny batteries to change.
  • Waterproof: Designed to handle water up to 1 meter deep.
  • Custom Fit: Made just for your ears for all-day comfort and security.

Starkey Edge AI

Starkey Edge AI hearing aids use AI to improve sound quality. A smart chip inside helps reduce background noise so speech is clearer.

The Edge AI automatically adjusts to different environments, making speech 30% clearer than older models. Whether you’re in a busy restaurant or at home, these hearing aids help you focus on conversations.

Unique Features:

  • Built to Last: Waterproof up to 1 meter and sweat-resistant.
  • Strong Bluetooth Connection: Works with smartphones and other devices. Supports Auracast™ for easy connection to TVs and public sound systems.
  • Health & Safety Features: Includes balance exercises and fall detection.
  • Easy App Control: The My Starkey app lets you adjust settings, control volume, stream calls, and find lost hearing aids. Works with smartphones and Apple Watch.

Starkey Genesis AI RIC

The Starkey Genesis AI hearing aids use advanced technology to help you hear soft sounds clearly, understand speech better, and reduce listening effort. The AI adapts to different listening situations, so you get the best sound possible.

Genesis AI receiver-in-the-canal (RIC) hearing aids work for mild to severe hearing loss. They come in rechargeable or disposable battery options. The rechargeable battery lasts up to 51 hours on a full charge and provides 3 hours of use with just a 7-minute charge.

Unique Features:

  • Waterproof: Special coating protects against sweat, rain, and dirt.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity: The My Starkey app lets you adjust settings, stream calls, and find lost hearing aids.
  • Remote Support: TeleHear allows remote adjustments through video chat.
  • Tinnitus Relief: Custom sound options help reduce ringing in the ears.

Starkey Genesis AI ITE

Starkey customizes its in-the-ear (ITE) hearing aids to fit each person perfectly. The Genesis AI ITE uses a smart processor that works like the human brain to provide natural sound in all environments.

These hearing aids work for mild to severe hearing loss. They are available in rechargeable and disposable battery options. The latest Genesis AI ITE model is the smallest wireless CIC hearing aid, providing a nearly invisible fit without losing sound quality.

Unique Features:

  • Waterproof: Special protective layers make them durable.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Connects to smartphones for easy control.
  • Remote Support: Adjustments can be made remotely through video chat.
  • Tinnitus Relief: Custom sound options help manage ringing in the ears.

Starkey CROS

Starkey’s CROS hearing aids help people who have hearing loss in one ear. These devices pick up sound from the weaker ear and send it to the better ear, making conversations easier and improving awareness in noisy places.

The CROS hearing aids work with the Genesis AI models and some older Starkey models.

Starkey Evolv AI BTE

Starkey’s Evolv AI behind-the-ear (BTE) hearing aids use smart sound processing to adjust to your surroundings. These hearing aids make 55 million automatic adjustments every hour and reduce unwanted noise, such as wind and background chatter.

Evolv AI BTE hearing aids work for mild to severe hearing loss. They come with rechargeable or disposable battery options.

Unique Features:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Connects with the Thrive Hearing Control app for easy adjustments.
  • Remote Support: Get hearing aid adjustments through video chat.
  • Tinnitus Relief: Custom sound programs help reduce ringing in the ears.

Starkey Evolv AI Power Plus BTE

The Starkey Evolv AI Power Plus BTE hearing aids offer all the features of the Evolv AI BTE but with extra power for people with severe to profound hearing loss.

These hearing aids use a size 13 disposable battery.

Unique Features:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Works with the Thrive Hearing Control app for easy settings.
  • Remote Support: Adjustments can be made remotely.
  • Tinnitus Relief: Custom sound settings to help reduce ringing in the ears.

Frequently Asked Questions About Starkey Hearing Aids

What are the tech levels of Starkey Genesis AI?

There are four technology levels: 24 (highest), 20, 16, and 12 (lowest). A CROS model is also available for people with severe hearing loss in one ear.

What app does Starkey Genesis AI use?

The My Starkey app connects with Genesis AI hearing aids. It lets you adjust settings, track your well-being, and find lost hearing aids.

How long do Starkey hearing aids last?

In-the-ear hearing aids usually last about five years, while behind-the-ear hearing aids last five to six years. How well you take care of them can affect their lifespan. Some people upgrade earlier to get the latest technology. An audiologist can help you decide when it’s time for a new hearing aid.
