Frequently Asked Questions

Can’t find the answers you’re looking for? Contact Us.

How is hearing loss diagnosed?

Hearing loss is diagnosed through a comprehensive hearing evaluation conducted by a hearing instrument specialist. Once testing is complete, the hearing aid specialists will explain the results, determine the type and severity of hearing loss, and recommend the best course of action, which may include hearing aids, medical treatment, or other hearing solutions.

What type of hearing aid do I need?

The best hearing aid for you depends on your hearing loss, lifestyle, and personal preferences. Hearing aids come in different styles, each offering unique benefits. A Hearing Instrument Specialist can help determine which style best meets your needs based on your hearing level, dexterity, lifestyle, and cosmetic preference.

How do I know if I have hearing loss?

Hearing loss often develops gradually, so it can be difficult to notice. Some common signs include:

  • Frequently asking others to repeat themselves
  • Struggling to hear in noisy environments
  • Increasing the volume on the TV or phone beyond what others find comfortable
  • Feeling like people are mumbling or speaking too softly
  • Difficulty hearing high-pitched voices, such as children or women
  • Experiencing ringing or buzzing in the ears (tinnitus)
  • Feeling tired from trying to listen and follow conversations

 

If you recognize any of these symptoms, scheduling a hearing evaluation with a hearing instrument specialist can help determine if you have hearing loss and what steps to take next. Early intervention can improve communication and overall well-being.
