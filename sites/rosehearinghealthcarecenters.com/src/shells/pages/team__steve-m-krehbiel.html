Steve M. Krehbiel

Hearing Aid Specialist SCHAS 0448

Steve M. Krehbiel is a hearing aid specialist providing hearing tests and hearing aids in Bluffton, SC.

Steve is a board-certified Hearing Aid Specialist with 38 years of experience in the hearing healthcare field. In addition to his extensive background, he is also a factory-trained repair specialist, bringing hands-on expertise to every patient he serves.

Originally from Camden, New Jersey, Steve spent over 20 years living near Utica, New York. He attended Kings College in Briarcliff Manor, NY, and later helped build a successful multi-office hearing practice in Roanoke, Virginia. Seeking warmer weather and a welcoming community, Steve relocated to Bluffton, South Carolina.

Steve is married and has two grown sons and three grandchildren. Outside of work, he enjoys spending time with his family, working on cars, carpentry projects, and relaxing at the beach.

With decades of experience and a passion for helping others, Steve looks forward to serving the hearing needs of the Bluffton community.
