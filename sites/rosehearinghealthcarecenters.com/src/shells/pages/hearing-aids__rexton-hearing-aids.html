Rexton Hearing Aids

Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers is an authorized provider of Rexton Hearing Aids in Bluffton, SC and Hilton Head Island.

Rexton is a well-respected hearing aid brand known for combining advanced technology with robust durability and user-friendly design. Rexton hearing aids are designed to support individuals in real-life listening environments—whether you’re in a busy restaurant, taking a phone call, or enjoying a walk outdoors.

Backed by decades of research and development, Rexton hearing solutions deliver natural, clear sound and helpful features that adapt to your life.

Current Rexton Hearing Aid Models in Bluffton, SC and Hilton Head Island

These are the hearing aid models currently available from Rexton.

Rexton BiCore R-Li and R-Li T (Receiver-in-Canal Models)

Rexton BiCore hearing aids offer faster sound processing, better background noise reduction, and improved battery life. These receiver-in-canal (RIC) models are sleek, powerful, and rechargeable—perfect for users who want comfort, modern technology, and a discreet design.

Unique Features:

  • BiCore Processing Platform: Delivers clean, clear sound with a fast response time for better speech understanding—even in noisy settings.
  • Rechargeable Battery: Long battery life with a full day of use on a single charge.
  • Motion Sensor Technology: Automatically adjusts to your movements and environment.
  • Bluetooth Connectivity: Direct streaming from iPhones and compatible Android™ devices.
  • Tinnitus Relief: Rexton offers optional tinnitus therapy tones to ease ringing in the ears.
  • IP68 Rating: Dust- and water-resistant for active lifestyles.

Rexton BiCore Custom Li (In-the-Ear Rechargeable)

BiCore Custom Li is Rexton’s rechargeable custom in-the-ear (ITE) solution for users who want a discreet yet powerful device that fits your ear’s unique shape.

Unique Features:

  • Custom-fit comfort: Tailored to your ear canal for a comfortable, secure fit.
  • Rechargeability: No need to change tiny batteries—just recharge overnight.
  • Streaming Capability: Enjoy Bluetooth audio streaming from smartphones and devices.
  • Tinnitus Management: Optional sound therapy support for tinnitus relief.
  • Easy Touch Control: Simple tap controls for volume and program changes.

Rexton BiCore BTE Models (M, P, HP)

The Rexton BiCore behind-the-ear (BTE) hearing aids offer powerful amplification for individuals with moderate to profound hearing loss. Available in three power levels—Mild (M), Power (P), and High Power (HP)—these devices provide strong performance in a durable package.

Unique Features:

  • BiCore Speech Enhancer: Reduces background noise and improves clarity in conversations.
  • Rechargeable or Disposable Battery Options: Choose what suits your lifestyle.
  • Telecoil Compatibility: Ideal for use in public venues with induction loop systems.
  • App Compatibility: Adjust volume, programs, and more through the Rexton App.

Rexton CROS/BiCROS Systems

For individuals with single-sided deafness, Rexton offers a CROS/BiCROS solution that transmits sound from the unaidable ear to the better-hearing ear.

Unique Features:

  • Seamless sound routing: Allows you to hear clearly from both sides.
  • Bluetooth Streaming: Stay connected while enhancing communication.
  • Compatible with Rexton BiCore RIC and BTE styles.

Rexton Accessories and Support Tools

Rexton offers helpful accessories to enhance your hearing experience:

  • Smart Transmitter 2.4 – Streams audio from your TV directly to your hearing aids.
  • Smart Mic – Remote microphone and Bluetooth streamer.
  • Smart Key – Simple remote control.
  • Charging Stations – Desktop and portable chargers for on-the-go power.
  • Rexton App – Adjust your settings, track battery life, and access remote support.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Rexton hearing aids last?

Most hearing aids, including Rexton, last around 5–7 years. This can vary based on how well they’re maintained. Our audiologists can guide you on whether your hearing aids are still meeting your needs or if it’s time to upgrade.

Are Rexton hearing aids rechargeable?

Yes. Rexton offers several rechargeable models, including BiCore R-Li and Custom Li options.

Can I stream phone calls or music through Rexton hearing aids?

Yes! Many Rexton hearing aids support Bluetooth streaming from iPhones and compatible Android phones.

Do Rexton hearing aids help with tinnitus?

Yes. Rexton includes tinnitus management features in many models, offering relief through soft therapy tones.

How do I adjust my Rexton hearing aids?

You can make simple adjustments using the Rexton App, Smart Key remote, or through remote support with your audiologist.
