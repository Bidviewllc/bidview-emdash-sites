Stay Connected with CaptionCall

Hearing loss can make everyday phone conversations frustrating or confusing. If you often miss words or struggle to keep up during calls, you’re not alone. That’s why Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers is proud to offer CaptionCall, a simple solution that makes phone calls easier and clearer for people with hearing loss.

What is CaptionCall?

CaptionCall is a phone service that helps people with hearing loss have better conversations. It uses advanced technology to provide real-time captions of what the caller is saying—displayed clearly on a large screen. This service is designed for individuals who need captions to understand phone conversations.

How CaptionCall Works

  • Easy-to-use amplified phone with a large display screen
  • Captions appear in real time as the caller speaks
  • Works with your landline or internet connection
  • Also available as a CaptionCall Mobile app for smartphones

Benefits of Using CaptionCall

  • Clearer phone conversations with loved ones, doctors, and more
  • Better confidence and independence when using the phone
  • Free service for individuals who qualify
  • No cost for the phone, captioning service, installation, or training

Who Qualifies for CaptionCall?

  • You must have hearing loss that makes it hard to use the phone
  • Our licensed audiologists can certify your hearing loss
  • Simple steps to qualify and begin using the service

How to Get a CaptionCall Phone

  1. Schedule a hearing evaluation at Rose Hearing Healthcare Centers
  2. Our team will confirm if you qualify and help with the certification
  3. The CaptionCall phone will be delivered and professionally installed
  4. A trained CaptionCall representative will teach you how to use it

CaptionCall and Your Audiology Care

CaptionCall works alongside your hearing aids to give you even better communication options. Our audiologists in Bluffton and Hilton Head Island are here to support your hearing health and help you stay connected in every part of your life.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is CaptionCall really free?

Yes! If you qualify, the phone, installation, and service are provided at no cost.

Do I need to be a certain age?

No—CaptionCall is available to people of all ages with qualifying hearing loss.

Can I use CaptionCall if I already have hearing aids?

Absolutely! CaptionCall works great with hearing aids to give you even more clarity.

What if I need help using the phone?

Free, in-home training is provided, and support is always available.
