Oticon Zeal

Oticon Zeal is the first hearing aid designed to do it all while staying completely unseen. Built for people who want powerful technology in an ultra-discreet design, Zeal delivers exceptional sound clarity, all-day comfort, and seamless connectivity in one tiny device no one will notice.

Zeal transforms the idea of what a hearing aid should be. Its small, nearly invisible design sits comfortably in your ear, giving you the confidence to live fully without drawing attention to your device. Powered by Oticon’s advanced BrainHearing™ technology, Zeal supports the brain’s natural way of processing sound. Enjoy clear, balanced, and comfortable hearing, even in noisy places.

Unique Features:

  • Made to Connect: Stream calls, meetings, music, and more directly from your phone, Apple Watch, TV, computer, or tablet. Zeal brings your digital world to life with smooth, high-quality wireless audio.
  • Reliable Rechargeability: No disposable batteries. With fast, dependable charging, you get all-day power that keeps up with your lifestyle.
  • Same-Day Fitting: Zeal is designed for convenience, offering the option for same-day fitting so you can begin hearing better right away.

Oticon Jet PX

Oticon Jet PX is uses advanced technology (called Deep Neural Network or DNN) to help you hear clearly. With features like Wind Noise Management and Speech Guard, it helps block out sounds that get in the way of talking to others. If you have ringing in your ears (tinnitus), Oticon Jet PX has built-in sounds to help you feel better. You can also connect it to your phone to listen to music, take calls, or change settings using the Oticon app. It comes in different colors and styles. It is also available in a rechargeable version.

Key Features:

  • AI-powered OpenSound Navigator with DNN: Improves hearing with clear, natural sound.
  • Tinnitus SoundSupport: Ideal for users with tinnitus as it gives access to customizable relief sounds.
  • SuperShield: Quickly adapts and prevents feedback before it occurs.

Oticon Own SI

Oticon Own SI hearing aids are tiny, comfortable devices that fit inside your ears and help you hear clearly again. It comes with two small styles to choose from – one that’s nearly invisible and another with easy-to-use buttons. These custom-made hearing aids are powered by BrainHearing technology to support how your brain naturally understands sound.

Key Features:

  • Discreet and sophisticated: Super small styles (IIC and CIC) that fit fully in your ear
  • BrainHearing™ technology: Helps your brain make sense of sound.
  • Custom-fit: Built for all-day comfort, even with glasses or masks.
  • Strong and durable: Water- and dust-resistant to last through busy days.

Oticon Intent

The Oticon Intent hearing aid is designed to adjust in real-time, even in noisy settings. It features 4D Sensor Technology, which tracks movement, conversation, and surrounding sounds to provide the clearest listening experience.

Using Deep Neural Network (DNN) technology, Oticon Intent mimics the brain’s natural way of processing sound, allowing you to focus on what matters most.

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth® LE Audio – Enables direct streaming from various devices.
  • Self-Calibrating Speaker – Adjusts sound for precise clarity.
  • Fast Charging – Charges 33% faster for all-day use.

Oticon Real

The Oticon Real hearing aid helps you handle everyday sounds, from sudden noises to background distractions like wind or hair movement. It uses DNN technology, trained with 12 million real-world sounds, to enhance speech clarity and reduce listening effort.

Available in miniRITE or miniBTE styles, Oticon Real fits mild to profound hearing loss and offers both rechargeable and 312 battery options.

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth Streaming – Works with both iPhone® and Android™ devices.
  • App Control – Adjust settings using the Oticon Companion or Oticon ON app.
  • Tinnitus Relief – Includes Tinnitus SoundSupport™.

Oticon Own

Oticon Own is custom-fit to your ear shape and lifestyle. It is the first in-the-ear hearing aid powered by DNN technology, making it easier to hear speech in complex environments.

Available in sizes for mild to severe hearing loss, with battery options including size 13, 312, or 10.

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth Streaming – Compatible with iPhone® and Android™ devices.
  • App Control – Adjust settings remotely via the Oticon Companion or Oticon ON app.
  • Tinnitus Relief – Offers Tinnitus SoundSupport™.

 

Note: The smallest models (CIC and IIC) do not support Bluetooth due to size limitations.

Oticon Zircon

Built with BrainHearing™ technology, Oticon Zircon delivers balanced sound and clear speech in noisy places. It scans your environment 500 times per second.

Available in miniRITE and miniBTE styles with 312 or rechargeable batteries.

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth Streaming – iPhone® and Android™ compatible.
  • App Control – Adjust settings via Oticon Companion or Oticon ON.
  • Tinnitus Relief – Tinnitus SoundSupport™ included.

Oticon CROS

For single-sided deafness, Oticon CROS transmits sound from the weaker ear to the stronger one. Works as CROS or BiCROS depending on your needs.

Available in rechargeable (CROS PX) or battery-powered models. Compatible with:

  • Oticon Real™
  • Oticon More™
  • Oticon Zircon
  • Oticon Opn S™
  • Oticon Play PX
  • Oticon Xceed

 

Key Features:

  • TwinLink™ Bluetooth – Stream while receiving sound from the weaker ear.
  • App Control – Use Oticon Companion or Oticon ON.
  • Tinnitus Relief – Tinnitus SoundSupport™ included.

Oticon Play PX

Oticon Play PX supports kids with clear, natural sound using DNN technology.

Available in miniRITE and miniBTE for slight to severe hearing loss. Choose from 312 or rechargeable batteries.

Key Features:

  • Tamper-Resistant – Secure battery for safety.
  • Water-Resistant (IP68) – Protection from moisture and dust.
  • LED Light Indicator – Displays battery status.
  • Bluetooth Streaming – Connects to iPhone® and Android™.

Accessories:

  • EduMic – For classroom clarity.
  • Amigo FM – Helps in noisy learning spaces.
  • ConnectClip – Streams voices up to 65 feet.

Oticon Xceed

Designed for severe to profound hearing loss, Oticon Xceed scans surroundings 100 times per second to clarify speech and reduce fatigue.

Behind-the-ear (BTE) with size 13 or 675 batteries.

Key Features:

  • Bluetooth Connectivity – Streams calls/audio to both ears.
  • App Control – Via Oticon Companion or Oticon ON.
  • Tinnitus Relief – Tinnitus SoundSupport™ included.

Frequently Asked Questions

How long do Oticon hearing aids last?

  • In-the-ear models: ~5 years
  • Behind-the-ear models: ~5–6 years

 

With good care, some last longer. Many users upgrade for new technology.

Are Oticon hearing aids compatible with Android and iPhone?

Staying connected is easier than ever with Oticon’s latest technology. Devices like the Intent, Real, and the new Zeal offer seamless pairing for both Apple and Android users through MFi and ASHA protocols. The newest 2026 releases also feature Auracast and Bluetooth LE Audio, which provide a more stable streaming experience and better battery longevity when used with modern mobile devices.

Which Oticon hearing aids support tinnitus relief?

Several Oticon hearing aids offer Tinnitus SoundSupport™, which plays soothing sounds like white noise or ocean waves. The following models include tinnitus relief:

  • Oticon Intent
  • Oticon Real
  • Oticon Own (except CIC/IIC)
  • Oticon Zircon
  • Oticon CROS
  • Oticon Xceed
  • Oticon Play PX (for children with tinnitus symptoms)

 

Use the Oticon Companion or Oticon ON app to customize tinnitus settings.
