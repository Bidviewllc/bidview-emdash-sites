Frequently Asked Questions

What is the top-of-the-line Signia hearing aid?

The Signia AX platform is the newest and most advanced. It’s used in Styletto, Pure, and Insio Charge&Go hearing aids. AX helps separate speech from background noise.

What are the levels of Signia Pure Charge&Go technology?

There are five levels: 1AX, 2AX, 3AX, 5AX, and 7AX. The higher the number, the more advanced the features.

Can I adjust my Signia hearing aid?

Yes! You can use the Signia app to change programs, volume, and even sound tone.
