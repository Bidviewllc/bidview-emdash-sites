Rebecca Vlk

AUDIOLOGIST

Dr. Rebecca Vlk (rhymes with Belk) obtained her clinical Doctorate in Audiology from East Carolina University in Greenville, North Carolina. She has worked for over 10 years in private practice in North Carolina. Dr. Vlk is a licensed audiologist in North Carolina and a fellow of the American Academy of Audiology, and is a member of the North Carolina Audiology Association.

Dr. Vlk is passionate about providing high-quality, individualized care to her patients and focuses on adhering to audiology best practices. She enjoys spending time on the personal, comprehensive assessment of hearing loss, and aural rehabilitation. Her favorite part about practicing audiology is helping her patients reconnect to the people and sounds they love.

In her free time, she loves to spend time with her family and her Jack Russel/Beagle mix, Rhodie. She also loves music and has sung in multiple community choirs.

STAYING CONNECTED

COMMUNITY OUTREACH & GIVE BACK

Caring for our community is an essential part of who I am.

Cary Audiology provides monthly services to area retirement communities, allowing residents to have easy access to hearing care. And, we are community care providers for the VA and work with the state to provide individuals with affordable hearing care for our Veterans.

We partner with Hearing The Call to provide hearing healthcare to individuals in need around the world.
