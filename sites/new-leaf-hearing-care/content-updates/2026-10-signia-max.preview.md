### Signia Pure Charge&Go MaX

Signia MaX (Multi-adaptive Xperience) is Signia's newest premium hearing aid platform, released in August 2026. It is powered by Acoustic Intelligence™, which runs four Deep Neural Networks at once: Speech Detection, Own Voice Detection, Scene Analysis, and Noise Processing. Together, they help wearers follow group conversations in noisy places without shutting out the rest of the room.

The first model on the platform is the Pure Charge&Go MaX, a rechargeable receiver-in-canal (RIC) hearing aid in eight colors. It comes with a pocket-sized portable charger that holds up to four full charges.

OneConnect Technology supports Bluetooth® Classic, LE Audio, telecoil, and Auracast™, so it works with nearly any phone or audio system.

Unique Features:

- RealTime Conversation Enhancement AI: Locates and enhances multiple conversation partners, so users can follow group conversations more easily.

- Own Voice Processing AI: Recognizes the wearer's own voice right away for natural sound from the first fitting, with no in-clinic calibration needed.

- Dynamic Noise Control AI: Detects what noise is present and where it comes from, then adjusts noise reduction based on whether the wearer is in a conversation.
