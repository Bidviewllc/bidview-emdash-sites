Hearing Loss

If you are experiencing hearing loss, we like to begin with our Functional Hearing Assessment. A beep test only tells us so much. This assessment goes much further, measuring how your brain processes sound, how it copes with noise, and how hard you're working to keep up. In just two hours, we can see the whole picture.

Tinnitus

If you hear ringing, buzzing, or hissing that no one else can, that's tinnitus. Most people are told there's nothing to be done—that you just have to live with it. That isn't true. Something can be done, and we're here to help.

Ear Spa (Earwax Removal)

Ears feeling clogged, muffled, or full? Sometimes, what looks like hearing loss is just wax—and it's never something to dig at with a Q-tip, which usually pushes it deeper. Our Ear Spa is a 30-minute appointment, and it's exactly as pleasant as it sounds.

Custom Molds &
Hearing Protection

We make all kinds of custom earpieces, each molded to fit your ear exactly. Some protect your hearing—on the job, at the range, at concerts. Others solve everyday problems: keeping water out when you swim, blocking noise when you sleep, or, if you're a musician, giving you a precise in-ear monitor fit. Whatever you need, a custom mold seals better and stays more comfortable than anything off the shelf.
