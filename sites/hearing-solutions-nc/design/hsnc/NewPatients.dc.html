Your First Visit

Plan on two hours with one of our doctors of audiology for your Functional Hearing Assessment. We'll start by looking in your ears to rule out anything simple, like wax or an issue we can fix on the spot. From there, we measure how you hear speech in background noise, how you tolerate sound, and how hard you're working to keep up, plus we'll provide a cognitive screening and establish a fall risk baseline.

If hearing aids make sense, you'll try them right here, and we'll retest with them in so you can hear the difference for yourself.

Hearing Aid Fitting

If we recommend hearing aids and you decide to move forward, we'll see you back in two to three weeks. We use that time to order your hearing aids and test them before you arrive. The fitting appointment is where they become yours. Live speech mapping and real-ear measurement let us check them against your prescription, in your ears, so soft sounds come through and loud sounds aren't too loud. We'll end the appointment by covering daily use and care.

[Additional details to come]

Check-Ins & Adjustments

The day after your fitting, we'll call to check in. You'll come in a week later, then usually two more times over the next two months as you get acquainted with your new way of hearing. Every visit is a chance to fine-tune how they sound at church, at dinner, in the car, or wherever you use them. We'll also give you some listening practice to do at home. Once you're as comfortable as you are confident, you've graduated!

[Additional details to come]

Ongoing Care & Support

After graduation, we'll see you every six months to check your programming, clean your hearing aids, and look at your wear time. Once a year, we repeat your hearing test, run diagnostics on your hearing aids, and reassess your fall risk. If something doesn't sound right in between, call us, and we'll get you in right away.
