About Hearing Solutions of North Carolina

You have our full attention.

Walking through our door can be the hardest part. It often means admitting something you've silently managed for years: the TV creeping louder, the smiling and nodding, the dinners out you've started to dread. If any of that sounds familiar, you're in good hands now.

When you come see us, we start by listening. Not the rushed kind of checklist listening, but the kind where we genuinely want to know what you've been missing and what you'd most love to hear again.

Then, we take the time to find the right solution.

“Right” isn't a figure of speech here. It's two hours of testing instead of fifteen minutes. It's measuring how your brain processes sound, not just whether your ears detect it. It's choosing from every major brand instead of the one we happen to contract with. It's staying with you forever, for every bit of fine-tuning you need along the way.

Because hearing isn't just sound. It's connection.
And that deserves our full attention.

You have our time.

You have our devotion.

We don't stop at “good enough.” We work until it's the best we can offer, and we don't disappear once we find it.

You have our word.

Our prices are published, plain as day, and our advice is honest. We'll never try to sell you something you don't need.

Hearing, shared.

If you end up needing hearing aids and choose our subscription plan, there's a built-in bonus for our community.

When your hearing aids come back to us at the end of your term, we send them to the manufacturer to be fully serviced, then pass them on through Hearing the Call—a nonprofit that gets working hearing aids to people right here who can't afford them. That way, the technology that brought your world back to you can do the same for a neighbor.
