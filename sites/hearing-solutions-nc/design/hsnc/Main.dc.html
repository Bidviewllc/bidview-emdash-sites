Is keeping up wearing you out?

Maybe you're focusing harder at dinner out, or asking people to repeat themselves. Maybe you're turning up the TV at night—even though the morning volume was fine. Or maybe it's easier to just tune out:
Smile. Nod. Hope for no questions. Repeat.

Hearing that takes work is hearing that needs help.

As hearing diminishes (even a little), your brain has to:

  • Read lips
  • Watch body language
  • Fill in missed words

That extra effort comes at a price: it's linked to social retreat, cognitive decline, and even higher risk of falls. The good news?

The earlier we catch hearing loss, the more we can do about it.

Don't smile and
nod your life away.

You have options (yes—plural). Come in, and we'll find out how your brain is actually processing sound. Then, we'll fine-tune a treatment plan for your ears and all you need them to hear, whether it's family dinners, work meetings, or the grandkids' soccer games.

What to expect.

Effortless hearing comes down to three deliberate steps.

  1. The effort test.

    A standard beep test only tells us so much. So our Functional Hearing Assessment goes further, measuring how your brain processes sound, how it copes with noise, and how hard you're working to keep up. In just two hours, we see the whole picture.

  2. The perfect match.

    From there, we prescribe what fits you. Maybe it's as simple as wax removal. If you need hearing aids, we're independent and free to select from all major manufacturers, so we can match the right devices to your brain, your anatomy, and the life you live.

  3. The out-of-the-drawer promise.

    With regular check-ins for comfort, programming, and any little thing that bugs you, there's no reason your new devices won't be on your ears doing the hard work of hearing 12+ hours a day.

So come sit with us for a couple of hours. We'll show you what's really going on, and if it makes sense, you will try hearing aids while you're here.
