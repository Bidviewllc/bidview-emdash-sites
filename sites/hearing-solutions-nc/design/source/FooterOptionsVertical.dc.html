Headers & footers

Footer options · vertical logo (F1–F7)

The same six footers with a stacked or tall logo. Each concept places the logo where a tall mark fits best.

F1 · Classic grouped The dependable default: brand card with call and Book, then three labeled link groups. Vertical logo gets its own narrow card.
F2 · Contact-led split Contact-led: a large Primary invitation panel with Book and Call, beside a compact contact and links card. Vertical logo sits beside the contact links.
F3 · Brand-led minimal Brand-led minimal on the Light colour: logo, one line, four links, phone and Book. Suits smaller sites; the most natural fit for a tall logo.
F4 · Patient-first Patient-first: links grouped by who is visiting, New patients and Current patients, in two task cards. Keep only links a practice really offers.
F6 · Multi-location Multi-location on Primary: brand and two link groups plus an outlined Find your office panel. Best past three offices.
F7 · Office cards (current) Office cards: brand card, one card per office with hours, and Explore. This is the footer on every page today.
