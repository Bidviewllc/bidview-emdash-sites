Sadie Mussler, Au.D is an audiologist providing hearing aids, hearing tests, and other audiology services in Salisbury, NC.

Dr. Sadie L. Mussler, originally from Raleigh, NC, received her Bachelor of Science degree in 2018, and her doctorate of Audiology in 2023. Dr. Mussler completed clinical rotations through a variety of different practice settings before joining Hearing Solutions in June 2023. Dr. Mussler is licensed by the state of North Carolina as an audiologist and dispenser of hearing devices. Dr. Mussler resides in Salisbury with her husband Michael, and two cats.
