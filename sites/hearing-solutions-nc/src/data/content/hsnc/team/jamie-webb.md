Jamie Webb received her Bachelor of Art in Communications in 1996 and her Bachelor of Science in Health Information Management in 1998. Jamie obtained her RHIA (Registered Health Information Administrator) certification in October of 1998. Jamie works part-time assisting at the front desk, scheduling patients, answering the phone, working check-in/check-out and completing reminder calls. She joins us with first-hand experience of the effects that hearing loss has on the family dynamics, as her mother currently uses hearing technology. Jamie is originally from Williamston, NC but currently resides in Salisbury with her husband John and their children Sutton and Lucas.
