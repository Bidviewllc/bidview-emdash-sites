Cheryl Keene is a Hearing Instrument Specialist providing hearing aids, hearing tests, and other audiology services in Salisbury, NC.

Cheryl Keene received her training and certification through the NC Association of Hearing Care Professionals in 2011 and her NC State Board License in 2012. Cheryl worked for Northeast Ear, Nose and Throat Center in Kannapolis, NC for 11 years before joining Hearing Solutions in April 2021. She and her husband live in Kannapolis, NC. They have 2 adult daughters and 3 beautiful grandchildren.
