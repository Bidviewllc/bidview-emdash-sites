Dr. Sierra Carpenter is an audiologist providing hearing aids, hearing tests, and other audiology services in Salisbury, NC.

Dr. Sierra D. Carpenter, originally from West Jefferson, NC, received her Bachelor of Science degree in 2020, and her doctorate of Audiology in 2024. Dr. Carpenter’s clinical experience ranges from newborn to geriatrics in a variety of clinical settings. Dr. Carpenter is licensed by the state of North Carolina as an audiologist to evaluate and manage hearing and balance disorders. Dr. Carpenter currently resides in North Wilkesboro with her husband and three dogs.
