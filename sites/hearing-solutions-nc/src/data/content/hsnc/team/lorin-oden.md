Lorin S. Oden, Au.D. is an audiologist and Dr. Cliff Approved Provider providing hearing aids, hearing tests, and other audiology services in Salisbury, NC.

Dr. Lorin S. Oden, originally from the Chicago area, received her Bachelor of Science degree in 1983, her Master's degree in Audiology in 1985 and her doctorate in 2009. Dr. Oden worked for a major hearing device manufacturer and Carolina ENT Specialists in Concord before opening Hearing Solutions of North Carolina. She is a Fellow of the American Academy of Audiology, Board Certified in Audiology, licensed by the state of North Carolina as an audiologist and dispenser of hearing devices. Dr. Oden resides on High Rock Lake in Salisbury with her husband Mark. The Odens have a son and daughter-in-law who live in Winston-Salem and a son who lives in Salisbury.
