On another note, we are glad to report in North Carolina, the sale of “locked” hearing aids is regulated by mandatory disclosure laws that took effect on October 1, 2025. Licensed audiologists and hearing aid specialists must inform buyers in writing if a device can only be serviced by specific providers.

Before completing the sale of a locked hearing aid, the seller must provide and have the buyer sign a written notice that states the device “can only be services or programmed at the specific facilities or locations”. This notice must disclose that the hearing aid uses either proprietary programming software or locked, nonproprietary programming software.

The legislation was created to increase transparency for consumers and to ensure they fully understand the limitations of a locked hearing aid before purchasing it. This is critical for consumer choice, as it prevents patients from being locked into a single provider for future adjustments and repairs.
